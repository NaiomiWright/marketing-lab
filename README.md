# Marketing Lab: a whole campaign in seven days

Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product.

**[Case study](https://naiomiwright.github.io/marketing-lab/case-study/)** · **[Landing page](https://naiomiwright.github.io/marketing-lab/)** · **[Results dashboard](https://naiomiwright.github.io/marketing-lab/dashboard/)**

## In thirty seconds

- **Who:** Naiomi Wright, brand and product marketer in animal health. [naiomiwright.com](https://naiomiwright.com)
- **What:** a full four-week Christmas campaign for a made-up dog chew, from brief to results, built in one week (16 to 22 November 2026).
- **How:** I made the marketing decisions. Claude Code, an AI tool that works on files on my computer, did the building.
- **Made up:** the brand, the product and every number. Nothing comes from any employer.

## The rules I worked to

- No medical claims, ever.
- Never invent a number. Every number here is made up and labelled as made up.
- Nothing private: no employer names, no real products, no real sales figures.
- Every public file carries the line: "Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product."
