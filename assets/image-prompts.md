# Image prompts: A calmer Christmas

Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product.

Three prompts to paste into ChatGPT, one at a time. They share one dog, one mood and one set of colours, so the images look like one campaign.

## How to use them

- Paste all three into the same ChatGPT chat, one after the other. This helps it keep the same dog.
- After prompt 1, if the dog changes, upload image 1 and say: "Use this exact dog."
- Save each image in `assets/images/`.

## The shared look

- **The dog:** Bramble, a young adult border collie. Black and white, with a white blaze down the face, a white chest and one ear that flops forward. Wears a plain forest-green fabric collar.
- **The mood:** warm, calm, cosy Christmas at home. A happy, settled dog. A busy, loving house in the background.
- **The colours:** forest green, warm cream, cranberry red, soft gold light, natural oak wood.
- **Never in the images:** text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names, packaging. Also no chocolate, grapes, raisins, mince pies, Christmas pudding, poinsettia or holly berries near the dog, as these are unsafe for dogs.

## 1. Hero image for the web page

Shape: wide landscape, 16:9.

```
Create a wide landscape image in a 16:9 shape, for the hero banner of a web page.

Scene: a cosy British living room on Christmas Day, in warm late-afternoon light. A young adult border collie called Bramble lies relaxed and content on a cream knitted blanket in front of a crackling fireplace. Bramble is black and white, with a white blaze down the face, a white chest and one ear that flops forward. Bramble wears a plain forest-green fabric collar. Bramble is gently holding a plain, natural-looking dog chew between the front paws, eyes soft and happy.

Background, softly out of focus: a decorated Christmas tree with warm gold fairy lights, a few wrapped presents in cranberry red and forest green paper, and a family laughing together on a sofa. The house feels busy and loving, but the dog is calm and at the centre of it all.

Layout: place Bramble in the right two-thirds of the frame. Keep the left third calmer and simpler (blanket, soft wall, gentle light), so a headline can be added later.

Colours: forest green, warm cream, cranberry red, soft gold light and natural oak wood. Warm, gentle, festive.

Style: realistic photograph, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, high quality.

Do not include any text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or packaging. Do not show chocolate, grapes, raisins, mince pies, Christmas pudding, poinsettia or holly berries near the dog.
```

## 2. Square image for social posts

Shape: square, 1:1.

```
Create a square image in a 1:1 shape, for Facebook and Instagram posts.

Use the same dog as before: Bramble, a young adult border collie. Black and white, with a white blaze down the face, a white chest and one ear that flops forward. Bramble wears a plain forest-green fabric collar.

Scene: Christmas morning at home. Bramble sits happily beside an opened present, with cranberry red wrapping paper and a forest green ribbon loosely around the paws. Bramble looks up towards the camera with a soft, happy expression, as if looking at a loved owner. In front of Bramble is a plain, natural-looking dog chew. Behind, softly out of focus, is a Christmas tree with warm gold fairy lights and a cream knitted throw on an oak floor.

Layout: Bramble in the centre of the frame, close up, filling about half the image. Simple, uncluttered background, so the image reads clearly on a phone screen.

Colours: forest green, warm cream, cranberry red, soft gold light and natural oak wood. Warm, gentle, festive. Same mood and colours as the first image.

Style: realistic photograph, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, high quality.

Do not include any text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or packaging. Do not show chocolate, grapes, raisins, mince pies, Christmas pudding, poinsettia or holly berries near the dog.
```

## 3. Banner for the top of an email

Shape: very wide and short, 3:1. ChatGPT may make it 3:2 instead. If so, crop it to 3:1 afterwards. The prompt keeps everything important in a central strip, so cropping is safe.

```
Create a very wide, short landscape image in a 3:1 shape, for a banner at the top of an email. If you cannot make 3:1, make the widest landscape image you can, and keep everything important in a narrow strip across the middle, so the top and bottom can be cropped off.

Use the same dog as before: Bramble, a young adult border collie. Black and white, with a white blaze down the face, a white chest and one ear that flops forward. Bramble wears a plain forest-green fabric collar.

Scene: a festive hallway at home on Christmas Day. Bramble lies calm and content on a cream rug by the front door, head resting on the paws, eyes soft. Coats and scarves hang on hooks, a few wrapped presents in cranberry red and forest green paper sit on an oak bench, and a garland of greenery with warm gold fairy lights runs along the wall. It feels like visitors have just arrived and the house is warm and happy.

Layout: Bramble on the left third of the frame. The rest of the frame is a soft, gently blurred run of garland and gold lights, so it stays calm and simple. Keep the dog fully inside the middle strip of the image.

Colours: forest green, warm cream, cranberry red, soft gold light and natural oak wood. Warm, gentle, festive. Same mood and colours as the first two images.

Style: realistic photograph, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, high quality.

Do not include any text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or packaging. Do not show chocolate, grapes, raisins, mince pies, Christmas pudding, poinsettia or holly berries near the dog.
```
