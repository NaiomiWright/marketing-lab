# Image descriptions

Short descriptions for people who cannot see the images. Use them as alt text on the web page, in emails and in social posts.

All three images were made with ChatGPT for this campaign. The dog is called Bramble.

## hero

Files: `hero.png` (full size), `hero-web.jpg` (1600 wide, for the web).

Alt text:

> A black and white border collie lies on a cream knitted blanket, holding a chew between its front paws and smiling at the camera. Behind it, a fire glows, a Christmas tree twinkles with gold lights, and a family laughs together on the sofa.

## social-square

Files: `social-square.png` (full size), `social-square-web.jpg` (1080 square, for social posts).

Alt text:

> A happy black and white border collie in a green collar lies on a wooden floor among torn red wrapping paper and green ribbon, with a chew between its paws. A Christmas tree with warm lights and wrapped presents glows behind it.
