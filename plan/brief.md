# Campaign brief: A calmer Christmas

Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product.

All numbers in this brief are made up for this portfolio campaign.

## The campaign on one page

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | Brackenfold Pet Health (made-up UK company) |
| Product | Brackenfold Settle, a made-up calming chew for dogs |
| Campaign | A calmer Christmas |
| Dates | Monday 23 November to Sunday 20 December 2026 (four weeks) |
| Market | UK only |
| Budget | £5,000 (made up) |
| Goal | 600 sign-ups for a free sample (made up) |

## What we want to be known for

- A warm, festive feel.
- The dog's interests at the heart of everything.
- Owners can enjoy Christmas Day without worrying about their dog.
- Dogs stay happy through visitors and a busy house.
- Owners can visit family, knowing their dog will be okay.

## Who we talk to

### Outside the company

**Dog owners**

- They want to give their dog a nice present.
- They want their dog to be safe.
- They do not want to leave their dog alone all day.
- They want something their dog can enjoy on Christmas Day.
- They do not want to worry.

Message: *A festive treat made for your dog, so you can both enjoy Christmas Day.*

**Vet practices that could stock it**

- No choking risk.
- A chew that is safe to eat.
- No upset stomachs or digestive problems.
- Nothing like the hard-to-digest chews already on the market.

Message: *Try Brackenfold Settle free this Christmas, and see if it suits the dogs you care for.*

### Inside the company

**Sales team of eight**

- Free samples to hand out.
- Hitting their targets.
- Being ready to answer any question.
- Clear product literature to back them up.

Message: *Everything you need to sell Settle: samples, clear literature and answers to the top questions.*

**Customer care team**

- Hearing about the campaign before customers do.
- Knowing the product well.
- Having samples ready.
- Knowing stock levels, so they do not oversell.
- Knowing how long new stock takes, if it sells out.

Message: *You hear it first: dates, stock levels and the sample process, before any customer calls.*

## Channels

We use these five channels and no others.

1. Email to dog owners
2. Email to vet practices
3. Facebook
4. Instagram
5. LinkedIn

Internal items (briefings and updates) are not campaign channels.

## Rules for all copy

- No medical claims. We do not say the chew treats, cures or prevents anything.
- We do not claim it reduces stress or anxiety.
- Any safety wording (choking, digestion) must be checked against product data before use.
- We do not name or criticise any other product.
