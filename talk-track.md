# Talk track: "How do you use AI?"

Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product.

What I say in an interview when they ask how I use AI. About 60 seconds out loud (about 160 words). Every line is from the Marketing Lab week. The brand and its numbers are made up.

---

So I use AI as my builder, and I stay the marketer.

In November I gave myself seven days to run a whole campaign for a made-up dog chew, using Claude Code, which is an AI tool that works on the files on my own computer.

I wrote the brief and chose the message for each audience, and it built the rest, so the Excel plan with real formulas, the emails and posts, the sales briefing and deck, a live landing page with tracked links and a results dashboard.

The part I am proudest of is the checking. I gave it rules on day one, no medical claims and never invent a number, and its brand review caught a hidden calming claim in my own launch email. It checked my numbers too. I said one channel cost nothing, but the cost was just missing from the results.

Then I turned the whole week into a skill, and I tested it on a second product. It built the plan and three emails in under five minutes.

So for me, AI does not replace my judgement, it just gives me the time to use it.

---

## If they ask a follow-up

- **"What did it get wrong?"** Some Excel steps failed before they worked, and one update left eight calendar rows blank. I only caught it because I check every day's work against a list.
- **"Can I see it?"** Yes. The case study, the landing page and the dashboard are all live, and the links are on my CV.
