## Rules for all copy

- No medical claims. We do not say the chew treats, cures, prevents or calms anything.
- We do not say it eases stiffness, pain or arthritis.
- We do not say, or hint, that it helps a cat move, jump or play.
- Any health question goes to the vet.
- We do not name or criticise any other product.
- Do not use the word "joint" in copy for cat owners until it is checked. Next to "older cats", it can read as a stiffness claim. (to confirm)
- Feeding guide, ingredients and any safety wording must be checked against product data before use. None was given in the brief.
- Read every piece as a whole. Lines that are fine alone can make a claim together.

## Name check

- **Ravenmoor Fireside (product name):** low risk. It makes no claim by itself. "Fireside" suggests warmth and rest. Do not pair it with pictures or words about comfort or easing. Keep it.
- **Good years together (campaign name):** some risk. Next to a joint supplement for older cats, it can read as a promise of more years, or better years. That would be a health claim. For this test, the campaign name stays in the plan only. It is left out of the emails.

Neutral options, if Naiomi wants to change it (to confirm):

1. Made for cats aged 10 and over
2. For our older cats
3. Ten and up

Web search, 22 November 2026: no pet brand or product called Ravenmoor or Ravenmoor Fireside was found. Results showed a game, a band, a theme-park ride and a US marketing agency called Ravenmoor. One UK pet trade business uses "Fireside" in its name (Field & Fireside Ltd, dog training equipment). It is not a cat supplement, but worth knowing before any real launch.
