# Short brief for the test run

Portfolio project by Naiomi Wright. Brackenfold Settle is a made-up product.
Ravenmoor Fireside is also a made-up product.

This is the short brief given to the campaign-kit skill to test it. The brand, the product and every number are made up.

- **Company:** Ravenmoor Pet Health, a made-up UK company.
- **Product:** Ravenmoor Fireside, a made-up daily joint supplement chew for older cats (aged 10 and over).
- **Campaign:** Good years together.
- **Dates:** Monday 1 February to Sunday 28 February 2027 (four weeks).
- **Market:** UK only.
- **Budget:** £4,000 (made up).
- **Goal:** 400 sign-ups for a free sample (made up).
- **Known for:** warm, calm, and on the side of older cats and the people who love them.
- **Outside the company:** owners of older cats; vet practices that could stock it.
- **Inside the company:** a sales team of six; the customer care team.
- **Channels:** email to cat owners, email to vet practices, Facebook, Instagram. No others.
- **Copy rules:** no medical claims. Do not say it eases stiffness, pain or arthritis, or that it helps a cat move, jump or play. Any health question goes to the vet. Do not name other products.
- **Make for this test:** the Excel plan and the three emails to cat owners only.

## Name check

On 22 November 2026, a web search for Ravenmoor found a game, a band, a theme-park ride and a new digital company. It found no pet brand or product called Ravenmoor or Ravenmoor Fireside.
